## 📖 About the Book

Some heroes wear capes.

Some carry swords.

Some become famous because they are stronger, faster, or braver than everyone around them.

**Gabe is not that kind of hero.**

Twelve year old Gabe simply wants to love Jesus more than popularity, approval, trophies, or comfort. Every ordinary day places another decision before him, inviting him to choose truth over compromise and faith over fear.

One honest choice during a science project begins a journey that reaches classrooms, friendships, families, and hearts, proving that one holy no can become someone else's first step toward Jesus.
