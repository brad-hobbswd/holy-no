A Christian Novel

Some heroes become famous because they are fearless. Some become strong because they never fail. Gabe becomes unforgettable because he loves Jesus more than popularity, approval, or comfort.

At twelve years old, Gabe discovers that the greatest battles are rarely fought with fists or loud voices. Every ordinary day presents another opportunity to choose truth over compromise, faith over fear, and obedience over acceptance.

One honest decision begins changing classmates, friendships, families, and hearts, proving that a single holy no can become someone else's first step toward Jesus.
